## Step 1 — Facebook + Instagram + ads (one step covers all three) · ~10 min

1. On a computer, go to **business.facebook.com** and log in with the Facebook account
   that manages the practice's Page.
2. Click **Settings** (gear icon) → **Business settings**.
3. In the left menu, click **Partners** → **Add** → **"Give a partner access to your
   assets."**
4. Paste our **Meta Business ID** (the number above) and click Next.
5. Share three things, ticking the "view performance / insights" style option for each:
   - your **ad account**
   - your **Facebook Page**
   - your **Instagram account**
6. Confirm. That's the biggest step done — and it took one screen.

> **If your Instagram is a personal account:** switch it first (free, 2 minutes):
> Instagram app → Settings → *Account type and tools* → *Switch to professional
> account* → Business. Then do step 1.
>
> **If anything on this screen looks unfamiliar or something's missing,** stop — don't
> wrestle with it. Book 15 minutes with us and we'll do it together: [BOOKING LINK]

## Step 2 — Google Ads (only if you run Google ads) · ~5 min

1. Log in at **ads.google.com**.
2. Find the **10-digit number** in the top-right corner (like 123-456-7890) and email it
   to us.
3. We'll send a connection request. When it arrives, go to **Admin → Access and
   security → Managers** and click **Accept**. Done.

## Step 3 — Google Analytics (if your website has it) · ~5 min

1. Log in at **analytics.google.com**.
2. Click **Admin** (gear, bottom-left) → **Property access management**.
3. Click **+ → Add users**, enter our reporting email (above), choose **Viewer**, and add.

*Not sure you have Google Analytics, or don't have the login? Just tell us — often the
website developer has it, and we'll chase them so you don't have to.*

## Step 4 — Your Google business listing · ~3 min

1. Go to **business.google.com** and pick your practice.
2. Open **Business Profile settings → People and access**.
3. Click **Add**, enter our reporting email, choose **Manager**, and invite.
   *(Google doesn't offer a view-only option here — Manager is the minimum. We only use
   it to read your performance data.)*

Reply to this email with what you completed and anything you got stuck on or don't have.
