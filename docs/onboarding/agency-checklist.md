> **Send to:** the client's existing marketing agency (Tier 1), CC the client.
> Fill in the two values below before sending. Agencies know these flows — this should
> take them 10–15 minutes total.

**To the team at [AGENCY]** — ROXIUM builds [PRACTICE NAME]'s client portal and
consolidates their cross-channel reporting. We need **read-only / analyst-level access**
to the accounts you manage for them. Nothing about your management access changes; we
will not modify campaigns, creatives, or settings.

**Grant access to:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Meta Business ID (partner access)** | `META_BUSINESS_ID` |
| **Google / all other platforms (email invites)** | `reporting@roxium.com` |

## Meta (one partner grant covers all three)

- ☐ Business settings → **Partners** → *Give a partner access to your assets* → enter the
  Business ID above, then assign:
  - ☐ **Ad account** — *View performance*
  - ☐ **Facebook Page** — view/insights-level (Analyst-equivalent)
  - ☐ **Instagram account** — *Insights*
    *(IG must be a professional account connected to the business portfolio — it already
    is if you're posting through Business Suite)*

## Google

- ☐ **Google Ads** — reply with the account's **10-digit customer ID** and we'll send a
  manager-account link request (you accept under Admin → Access and security →
  Managers). *Or* invite the email above directly with **Read-only** access.
- ☐ **GA4** — Admin → Property access management → add the email above as **Viewer**.
- ☐ **Google Business Profile** (if you manage it) — People and access → add the email
  above as **Manager** (Google offers no view-only role; we use it for performance data
  only).

## If applicable

- ☐ **YouTube** — Studio → Settings → Permissions → add the email above as **Viewer**.
- ☐ **Microsoft Ads** — Account access → invite the email above as **Viewer**.
