# Tier 3 Live Onboarding Call — 30-Minute Script

**Use when:** nobody at the practice confidently owns the marketing accounts. The goal is
to leave the call with Meta partner access granted, the Google situation mapped (granted
live if possible), and every remaining item assigned to a named person with a date.

**Before the call (5-min prep, ROXIUM):**
- ☐ ROXIUM Business ID and reporting email pasted at the top of your notes, ready to
  drop in chat.
- ☐ Client's checklist pre-filled with what we already know (from the kickoff call /
  their website / their ad presence).
- ☐ Practice already created in the portal so you can show the dashboard at the end.
- ☐ Ask in the invite: *"Please join from a computer (not a phone) and have whoever
  might know the Facebook/Instagram logins available."*

**Ground rules for you:** the client shares *their* screen and does the clicking — we
never take their credentials or drive their session with their passwords. You navigate by
voice. Reassure constantly: nothing we do today lets ROXIUM post, spend, or change
anything.

---

## Minute 0–3 · Frame the call

> "Thanks for making time. In the next half hour we're going to connect your marketing
> accounts to your ROXIUM portal. Two things I want you to hear up front: **you will
> never share a password with us** — every platform has a built-in way to give us
> read-only access — and **you stay in full control**; you can remove our access anytime
> with one click. Our job today is just to flip those switches together. Sound good?"

Then the routing question, asked precisely:

> "Who currently has the ability to log in to your Facebook or Instagram — you, someone
> in the office, or an outside company?"

Write down every name/company mentioned. This is your map for minutes 5–25.

## Minute 3–5 · Inventory

Rapid-fire, yes/no, fill the checklist as they answer:

- "Do you run any paid ads right now — Facebook/Instagram? Google?"
- "Is your Instagram a business account?" (If they don't know: "Open Instagram — does
  your profile show 'Professional dashboard'?" That's the tell.)
- "Do you have a Facebook business Page for the practice?"
- "Does your website have Google Analytics on it, as far as you know?"
- "When people Google the practice, is that Google listing something you manage?"

## Minute 5–20 · Meta (the anchor — spend the time here)

Have them share screen and go to **business.facebook.com**.

**Branch A — they land in a Business Suite for the practice (best case):**
1. "Top-left — confirm it says the practice name. Great, that's your business portfolio."
2. "Click the gear → Business settings. If you don't see it, tell me what you do see."
   (If lost, paste `business.facebook.com/settings/partners` in chat.)
3. "In the left menu find **Partners** → **Add** → **Give a partner access to your
   assets**."
4. "I'm pasting our Business ID in the chat — copy it into that box."
5. Walk assets one at a time: "Select your **ad account** → turn on **View
   performance**. Now **Pages** → your Page → the view/insights permission. Now
   **Instagram accounts** → your account → **Insights**."
6. "Hit confirm. Done — that's the whole Meta side."
7. You verify live in ROXIUM's Business Manager that the assets arrived. Say so out loud:
   *"I can see it on my side — you're connected."* (Small moment, big trust.)

**Branch B — no Business Manager / IG is personal / assets missing:**
- IG personal → convert live: Instagram app → Settings → Account type and tools →
  Switch to professional account → Business. (2 minutes, do it now.)
- No business portfolio → create one live at business.facebook.com (name, their email —
  5 minutes), then connect the Page and IG to it, then run Branch A.
- Page/ad account owned by an old agency or web person → **don't burn the call on it.**
  Note who owns it, and make it the #1 follow-up action: "We'll contact them with you
  CC'd and get it transferred." Continue with whatever assets they *do* control.

## Minute 20–26 · Google

Triage — do live what's quick, assign the rest:

- **Google Ads** (if they run it): "In Google Ads, top right, there's a 10-digit customer
  ID — read it to me." We send the manager-link request after the call; tell them an
  'accept' prompt will appear under Admin → Access and security → Managers, and they just
  click Accept. (If they're logged in and comfortable, do the direct Read-only email
  invite live instead — 2 minutes.)
- **GA4**: if they can log in to analytics.google.com now: Admin → Property access
  management → add our reporting email as **Viewer**. If they can't, find out who can
  (web developer?) and assign it.
- **Google Business Profile**: "Google doesn't have a read-only role here, so the minimum
  is 'Manager' — we'll only use it for performance data." business.google.com →
  People and access → add our email as Manager. If the profile is unclaimed or owned by
  an old vendor, note it as a follow-up.

## Minute 26–30 · Show the payoff, assign the leftovers

1. Share **your** screen. Open their ROXIUM portal (Preview as client): "Here's where all
   of this lands — your roadmap, your videos, your deliverables, and within a couple of
   days, your live marketing numbers, all platforms in one place. This is the one link
   you'll ever need to check."
2. Read back the open items, each with a name and a date: *"Jessica is finding the GA4
   login by Thursday; we're emailing your old agency about the Page today."*
3. Close: "You'll get your portal invite by email today — it's a sign-in link, no
   password to create. Anything still missing, we'll chase — you don't have to project-
   manage this."

**After the call (same day, ROXIUM):**
- ☐ Send the recap email: what got connected, open items with owners/dates, their portal
  invite, our Business ID + reporting email for anyone who still needs them.
- ☐ Wire the granted sources (Coefficient → tabs → portal → Sync now) and flip the
  Marketing Connections card.
- ☐ Put open items in the Needs-Attention queue; follow up in 3 business days.
