# Demo Video Script — "Connecting Your Accounts to ROXIUM" (2–4 min)

**Purpose:** the reusable screen recording linked from the Tier 2 client checklist (and
the Tier 1 agency email). It shows the Meta partner grant end-to-end and explains *why*
it's safe. Record once, host at a stable link, update only when Meta moves the UI.

**Recording setup:** screen-record a demo/sandbox Business Suite (never a real client's),
1080p, cursor highlighted, calm pace. Keep every shot to one action. Voice: warm,
unhurried, zero jargon — the viewer is an office manager, not a marketer. Blur/replace
any real IDs except ROXIUM's own Business ID, which should be clearly readable and also
shown as an on-screen text overlay.

---

## Scene 1 — Why you're watching this (0:00–0:25)

*Visual: ROXIUM portal dashboard — KPI cards, roadmap, video pipeline.*

> "Hi — this short video shows you how to connect your practice's marketing accounts to
> your ROXIUM portal, so your Facebook, Instagram, and ad results show up here
> automatically, next to everything we're building for you.
>
> Before we start, the most important thing: **you will never give us a password.**
> We use Facebook's official partner feature, we only get **read-only access** to your
> numbers, and you can remove us anytime with one click. Total time: about five minutes."

## Scene 2 — Business settings → Partners (0:25–1:05)

*Visual: business.facebook.com → gear → Business settings → Partners.*

> "On a computer, go to **business.facebook.com** and sign in with the Facebook account
> that manages your practice's Page. Click the **gear icon** for Settings, then
> **Business settings**. In the menu on the left, find **Partners** — and click **Add**,
> then **'Give a partner access to your assets.'**"

*(On-screen tip overlay: "Can't find it? Use this direct link:
business.facebook.com/settings/partners")*

## Scene 3 — Enter ROXIUM's Business ID (1:05–1:30)

*Visual: the partner ID field; the ID shown large as an overlay.*

> "It asks for a Partner Business ID. That's **our** ID — it's in the email we sent you,
> and it's on screen now. Paste it in and click Next. This is how Facebook knows exactly
> who you're sharing with — ROXIUM's business account, not a person."

## Scene 4 — Share the three assets (1:30–2:30)

*Visual: assign ad account → View performance; Page → view/insights; Instagram →
Insights. One asset per shot.*

> "Now you choose what to share and how much. Three things:
>
> First, your **ad account** — and here's the key part — turn on only **'View
> performance.'** That means we can see results, but we can't touch your ads or your
> billing.
>
> Second, your **Facebook Page** — same idea: the view-and-insights level, nothing that
> lets anyone post as your practice.
>
> Third, your **Instagram account** — tick **Insights**.
>
> Then hit **Confirm** — and that's it. One screen, and Facebook, Instagram, and your
> ads are all connected."

## Scene 5 — Control + the payoff (2:30–3:15)

*Visual: the Partners screen showing ROXIUM listed (control), then cut back to the portal
dashboard filling with data.*

> "One more thing, because it matters: on this same Partners screen you can always see
> exactly what ROXIUM has access to — and remove it anytime. You're in control,
> permanently.
>
> Within a couple of days, your portal starts showing your real numbers — every channel,
> every month, right next to your roadmap and your videos. One login, one place, and
> we handle all the reporting setup from here.
>
> If anything on your screen looked different from this video, don't fight it — grab a
> 15-minute slot with us at the link below and we'll do it together. See you in the
> portal."

*(End card: ROXIUM logo · Business ID · reporting email · booking link.)*
