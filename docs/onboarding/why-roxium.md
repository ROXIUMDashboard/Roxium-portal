# Why ROXIUM — vs. "I already have Meta Business Suite"

**Client-facing.** Use this verbatim in emails, on calls, or on the site when a client
(reasonably) asks: *"Meta already shows me views, reach, and my ad results — why do I
need the ROXIUM portal?"*

---

Meta Business Suite is genuinely good — at showing you Meta. That's exactly the
limitation:

- **Meta Business Suite** shows Facebook and Instagram. Not your Google Ads, not your
  website analytics, not your Google reviews traffic.
- **Google Ads** shows Google Ads.
- **Google Analytics** shows website behavior.

Each one is a keyhole view of one channel, in that platform's own language, with that
platform's own definitions. To see how your marketing is *actually* doing, someone has to
log in to three or four dashboards, export numbers, and stitch them together — and none
of those dashboards knows anything about the work being done on your behalf.

**Your ROXIUM portal is the one operating view of the whole engagement:**

1. **All channels, one screen.** Meta, Google Ads, analytics, YouTube — standardized to
   the same metrics (spend, reach, clicks, cost-per-click…) so months and channels are
   actually comparable, with every past month preserved as a locked snapshot.
2. **Numbers next to the work.** Ad performance sits beside your roadmap phase, your
   deliverables, and your video pipeline — so "reach was up 27%" appears next to *why*
   (the campaign we launched, the videos that went live).
3. **What needs attention next.** The portal isn't a rear-view mirror; it shows current
   phase, what's in progress, what's coming, and what we need from you.
4. **One login for your whole team.** You and your staff get your own secure sign-in
   (no passwords to manage — a link in your email). Everyone sees the same truth.
5. **Zero maintenance on your side.** We handle all reporting setup. We only ever ask
   for **read-only access** through each platform's official sharing feature — you never
   share a password, and you can revoke our access anytime.

**The short version:** Meta Business Suite tells you how your Facebook and Instagram are
doing. Your ROXIUM portal tells you how your *marketing* is doing — every channel, every
deliverable, every next step, in one place built specifically for your practice.
