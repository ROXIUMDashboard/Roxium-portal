The Problem

Every dashboard you already have shows one slice.

Each platform reports on itself, in its own language, with its own definitions. None of them knows what work is being done, what's late, or whether the numbers are even syncing.

Meta Business Suite

Facebook and Instagram only. Nothing about your Google Ads, your website behavior, or the campaign work behind the numbers.

Google Ads & GA4

Google's channels and your site — in metrics that don't line up with Meta's, and with no view of deliverables or delivery status.

Agency slide decks

A PDF once a month. Static, curated, already out of date — and disconnected from what happens next.

ROXIUM instead

One operating layer above your platforms.

ROXIUM standardizes every channel to the same metrics, freezes each month as a locked snapshot, and puts performance beside the roadmap, deliverables, and videos that produced it — with the current month updating live.

  • All channels, one screen, comparable metrics
  • Numbers next to the work that drove them
  • Past months archived, current month live
  • What needs attention, surfaced automatically
The Platform

Visibility

Onboarding

Connected in days. Without a single password.

Every platform has an official way to grant read-only access — that's all we ever use. Tell us who manages your marketing today, and we handle the rest.

10–15 minTier 1

You have an agency

  1. We email your agency a one-page checklist.
  2. They grant partner access — they do this all the time.
  3. Your data starts flowing. You do nothing.
15–30 minTier 2

Someone in-house runs it

  1. We send them a plain-language checklist and a 3-minute video.
  2. One screen in Meta connects Facebook, Instagram, and ads at once.
  3. Stuck anywhere? We jump on a 15-minute screen-share.
30–45 minTier 3

Not sure who owns what

  1. We book one guided call and walk it together.
  2. You click, we navigate — your logins never leave your hands.
  3. Anything missing, we chase down for you afterward.
You stay in control, permanently. Access is granted through each platform's own partner/user feature at view-only level, visible on your side at all times — and revocable with one click. We can see performance; we can't post, spend, or change anything.
