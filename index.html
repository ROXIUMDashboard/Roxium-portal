Clients & access

Add a new client practice (it's seeded with the standard deliverables, roadmap and pipeline), then invite the surgeon and their staff. Invited users get a sign-in link by email; access is invite-only — nobody can self-register.

Monthly KPIs

Each month is its own saved snapshot — editing one month never changes another, and re-reporting a later month can't overwrite the past. Pick the month, enter values, Save.

Deliverables, roadmap & video pipeline

Deliverables: edit any field, dropdown to change status, ✕ to remove, "+ Add" for new ones. Roadmap: the date under each milestone is editable (dates are set automatically per practice at kickoff — change one only if a date shifts). Video pipeline: drag a card between stage columns to move it, or click a card to rename it / flag it blocked. The surgeon sees clean, read-only versions.
